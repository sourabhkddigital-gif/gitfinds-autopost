Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

GitFinds Autopost ("the app") is an internal tool operated by the GitFinds team at KD Digital to publish our own videos.

What the app accesses

  • Google Sheets / Google Drive: reads our content schedule and the video files listed in it, and updates the posting status in that sheet.
  • YouTube: uploads our videos to our own GitFinds channel.
  • Instagram: publishes our videos to our own @gitfinds account.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish our own content. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person, and does not use data for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets. Videos are downloaded temporarily only while posting and deleted right after.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in Instagram settings.
